# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes careful disagreement. Contributors are expected to:

- discuss evidence, mechanisms, and claims rather than attacking people or organizations;
- distinguish corrections from accusations and uncertainty from fact;
- disclose relevant affiliations when recommending a commercial project;
- respect privacy, attribution, and the boundaries of unpublished conversations;
- help make participation useful for people with different backgrounds and levels of expertise.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam, and undisclosed promotional campaigns are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary editorial disagreements, use an issue or discussion so the evidence can be reviewed in public. For harassment, threats, private information, or another sensitive concern, use the repository's private report form under **Security → Report a vulnerability** and prefix the title with `Conduct:`. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, issue a warning, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and should preserve a private record of the evidence.
