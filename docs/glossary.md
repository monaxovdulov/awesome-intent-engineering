# Glossary

## Intent

A human or organizational commitment about an outcome, rationale, constraint, trade-off, non-goal, authority boundary, or definition of done.

## Intent Engineering

The emerging practice of making intent explicit, durable, traceable, usable by agents, and verifiable against evidence.

## Intent-Driven Development (IDD)

A family of approaches that keeps the why and what-to-achieve as durable source artifacts and derives or constrains downstream specification and implementation work from them.

## Intent debt

The absence or erosion of explicit rationale, goals, and constraints required by future humans and agents to change a system safely. See https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22106.

## Intent loss

A practitioner term for the disappearance of original requirements or rationale into generated code, transient chat history, or stale documents.

## Intent drift

Divergence between intended outcomes or constraints and the behavior produced by an agent or implementation.

## Intent diff

A review artifact showing which goals, requirements, constraints, or decisions were added, removed, refined, or superseded.

## Intent coverage

A measure of how much declared intent is connected to implementation and verification evidence. The exact denominator and semantics are not standardized.

## Intent fidelity

The degree to which an output preserves the relevant dimensions of the originating intent.

## Living intent

A durable record attached to a capability, product area, or system that changes as decisions and implementation evolve.

## Change spec

A bounded artifact describing a particular change. It may remain as history after completion but is not necessarily maintained as the current description of the feature.

## Evidence anchor

A link from an observation, quote, metric, or other evidence item to the outcome, edge case, or decision it supports.

## Finding

A result from implementation, testing, review, or production that confirms, weakens, or contradicts an intent assumption.

## Provenance

Metadata identifying the source, author, time, approval state, and derivation path of an intent item, requirement, explanation, or evidence item.

## Rejected path

An approach that was attempted or considered and deliberately abandoned, together with the reason and conditions under which it may be reconsidered.

## Assurance closure

A proposed state in which a system establishes what must be true, obtains appropriate evidence, assesses credibility, preserves validity, and uses remaining uncertainty to bound agent authority.

## Agentic entropy

A research term for accumulating divergence between agent actions and architectural intent across a software-development process.
